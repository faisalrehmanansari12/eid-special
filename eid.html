Chaand Raat, But My Moon Is Missing

چاند کے بنا چاند رات کیسی؟

Tomorrow is Eid, and tonight carries the beauty of Chaand Raat — lights, prayers, smiles, celebration, and warmth. But my heart keeps asking one simple thing:

How can my Chaand Raat feel complete when my moon is not smiling with me?

My Eid Wish

If Eid is about happiness, then you are my happiness. If Chaand Raat is about light, then you are the light my heart searches for. And if tonight is a night of prayers, then my prayer is for us.

May Allah remove the heaviness between us. May He put softness back into our words. May He protect the love we have, and may this Eid bring us closer instead of letting silence pull us apart.

For My Chaand

The moon may decorate the sky tonight,
but the light I search for still lives in you.

Eid feels beautiful to the world,
but my heart only asks for your smile.

Some people wait for the moon to appear,
I wait for my Alishba to smile again.

My Little Chaand Raat Confession

My darling meri, my cutie, my ladoo, my kadoo, my danedaar, my tharki junior — even when there is silence between us, you are still the first person my heart wants to reach.

I do not want this Eid to begin with distance. I want it to begin with a softer heart, a calmer conversation, and maybe one little smile from you that makes everything feel alive again.

Eid Mubarak, My Alishba

May this Eid bring us peace, love, forgiveness, and one more beautiful beginning.
