Where everything slowly began

Our Story

Some stories do not begin loudly. Some begin with one glance, one corridor, one quiet habit of looking for the same face again and again.

Chapter 01

The First Glimpse

I still remember the first time I saw you in the department corridor at university. I was from the 21 AI batch, and you were from the 24 AI batch — new, graceful, and somehow impossible to ignore.

That first glance stayed with me. I do not know how to explain it perfectly, but I know this: after seeing you, my eyes had learned a new habit — to search for you.

Chapter 02

The Welcome Party

Then came the welcome party for the 24 batch. You were shining that day in a way I still remember. There were many people around, but my eyes were looking for only one person.

Slowly, secretly noticing you became a part of my routine. I would see you quietly, and even those little moments started feeling special to me.

Chapter 03

The Instagram Excuse

I wanted to talk to you, so I found a small excuse. I asked you to send me the welcome party pictures and videos on Instagram.

On the outside, it looked like a simple request. But for me, it was a way to create a connection. And slowly, talking to you started feeling nice.

Chapter 04

The Little Moments

I used to encourage you to be strong. When you walked with your head down, I would tell you to walk with your head held high.

And yes, sometimes I teased you too — like telling you that juniors should greet their senior. You did it once, maybe out of fear, maybe out of innocence, but I still remember that small moment.

Chapter 05

The Pen Incident

Then came that exam day at the canteen. My friend needed a pen, and I saw you with your friends. I came to you thinking it would be simple because we were already talking.

But later, I understood that the situation became uncomfortable for you because your friends started teasing you. At that time, I did not fully know what you felt. Now I understand it better, and I value your feelings more.

Chapter 06

The Silence

After that, there was distance. Conversations faded, and for a while, things became quiet between us.

But even in that silence, some part of me still wanted to talk to you again. So I tried through small things — reels, reactions, and little chances that might open the door again.

Chapter 07

When Conversation Returned

Then one day, you asked me for help with calculus and for a YouTube channel for exam preparation. Maybe for the world it was just a study question.

But for me, it felt like conversation had found a way back. I teased you, helped you, and somewhere inside, I became very happy again.

Chapter 08

The Farewell

Then came my farewell. Four years of my bachelor’s journey were ending, and you arrived looking so beautiful that I still remember that moment clearly.

You had a charm that day that stayed in my eyes. And then, somehow, you appeared in one of my pictures in the background. That little coincidence became another sweet excuse for us to talk.

Chapter 09

From Talking To Loving

After that, we slowly started talking almost every day. And without either of us fully realizing when it happened, feelings started growing.

I thank Allah for placing this love between us. Today, even before our official forever, our emotions feel deep, serious, and real.

Chapter 10

The Family Feeling

One of the most beautiful things for me is that my mother, my two younger sisters, and my little brother know about you. My mother and sisters like you, and seeing that bond makes me genuinely happy.

When I see that connection, my eyes light up. It feels like Allah has shown me a glimpse of the complete family my heart wants.

Chapter 11

My Prayer

What more could a person ask for than a love that feels like home and a future that already carries the warmth of family?

My prayer is simple: may Allah always keep us together, keep our hearts soft toward each other, and never let distance become stronger than the love He placed between us.

This Story Is Still Ours

I do not want one fight to become bigger than everything we have lived, felt, and prayed for.
